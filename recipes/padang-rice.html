Padang Rice

Padang Rice from Sumatera, Indonesia

Padang Rice is always my favorite food since in campus.

The standout ingredients was always about the soup or sauces. A blend of coconut oil, spicy chilly and organic soup. Not to mention the affordability of the food.

Ingredients
  • Rice
  • Beef Rendang
  • Chicken Rendang
  • Vegetables
  • Spicy Soup
Steps
  1. Prepare the rice
  2. Prepare the curry chicken
  3. Prepare the chicken rendang
  4. Prepare the vegetables
  5. Prepare the spicy soup
  6. Mix all ingredients and enjoy!
