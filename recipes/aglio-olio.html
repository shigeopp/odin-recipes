Aglio Olio is my favorite creamy pasta

Aglio Olio from Italy

Aglio Olio is a traditional Italian cuisine. The seasoning is famously incorporated with garlic. Taste so great!

Ingredients
  • Pasta
  • Onion
  • Olive Oil
  • Garlic
  • Salt and Pepper
  • Water 300ml
Steps
  1. Prepare the pasta into a pot filled with the water
  2. Boil it good for 10"
  3. Prepare the sauce
  4. Mix onion, garlic, salt and pepper, and olive oil
  5. Pour the pasta into the mixed and smooth sauce
  6. Enjoy!
